Project Aims & Clinical Motivation

Diagnostic Triage Acceleration

Chest radiography accounts for over 40% of all medical imaging globally. Radiologist workload and diagnostic backlogs create critical delays in emergency departments. Our system automates rapid pre-screening to triage high-risk cases in under 1.2 seconds per scan.

Pneumothorax Boundary Delineation

Pneumothorax carries severe inter-observer diagnostic variability (up to 30% disagreement among general radiologists for subtle apical air). Our scSE-enhanced UNet++ segments exact pleural lines, assisting clinicians in needle thoracostomy and tube drainage decisions.

Explainable Radiology Synthesis

Addressing the 'black box' problem in medical AI by integrating pixel-level Grad-CAM heatmaps, boundary masks, and natural language report generation (Findings & Impression) with transparent learned attention weights (Global vs Local vs Context).

Training Methodology & Engineering Pipeline

MODULE 1 · CLASSIFICATION

EfficientNetV2-S + Grad-CAM

Trained using progressive resolution scaling (224×224 to 384×384) with RandAugment and CLAHE preprocessing. Class imbalance tackled via weighted focal binary cross-entropy (2.89× penalty on false negatives).

  • Optimizer: AdamW (lr=2e-4, weight_decay=1e-2)
  • Schedule: Cosine Annealing with 5 warmup epochs
  • Explainability: Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM) from stage-7 conv features
  • Outcome: 93.11% Test Accuracy, 0.9840 ROC-AUC
MODULE 2 · SEGMENTATION

UNet++ with EfficientNet-B3 & scSE

Nested dense skip pathways aggregate multi-scale features. Concurrent spatial and channel Squeeze-and-Excitation (scSE) blocks re-calibrate feature maps to suppress thoracic background noise.

  • Loss: Compound Loss = 0.5×Dice Loss + 0.5×SoftBCE
  • Post-Processing: Systematic threshold sweep finding optimal τ = 0.30 (vs default 0.50)
  • Noise Filter: Connected components min-area filter (600 px) eliminating false positive speckles
  • Outcome: 0.8407 Mean Dice, 0.8246 IoU, 0.9771 Specificity
MODULE 3 · REPORT SYNTHESIS

Dual-Branch Swin-T + DistilGPT-2

Novel dual-pathway vision encoder: a global Swin Transformer captures global thoracic geometry, while a dense CNN pyramid extracts local opacity features, fused via adaptive attention gating.

  • Learned Gate Weights: α (Global) = 60.3%, β (Local) = 33.7%, γ (Context) = 6.0%
  • Decoder: Autoregressive DistilGPT-2 with causal self-attention and cross-attention
  • Inference: Beam Search (width k=4) with repetition penalty and n-gram blocking
  • Outcome: BLEU-1 0.3230, METEOR 0.2899, ROUGE-L 0.2894

Future Scope & Clinical Translation Roadmap

PHASE I

PACS & DICOM Integration

Direct integration into hospital Picture Archiving and Communication Systems (PACS) using DCMTK/Orthanc servers with HL7 and FHIR communication standards for automated worklist feeding.

PHASE II

Multi-Pathology Expansion

Expanding from pneumonia and pneumothorax to all 14 common thoracic conditions on the NIH ChestX-ray14 benchmark (cardiomegaly, atelectasis, pleural effusion, nodule, mass, consolidation).

PHASE III

Edge AI for Rural Clinics

Model quantization (INT8/FP16) via ONNX Runtime and TensorRT to run on low-power local workstations in primary health centres across remote mountainous regions without requiring internet access.

PHASE IV

Clinical Trial & CDSCO Approval

Conducting prospective observer-blinded clinical validation trials with thoracic specialists at regional government hospitals and pursuing Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO) Class C medical device clearance.
